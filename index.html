PACRA · Company

For a private company limited by shares, PACRA's service guidance describes a Companies Form 3 application, at least two directors, and additional company documents depending on the Articles selected.

PACRA · Fees

PACRA publishes a live forms-and-fees catalogue. Fees differ by service and may change, so this demo links to the current official catalogue instead of guessing a total.

ZRA · Company TPIN

ZRA says that for businesses registered with PACRA after 2020, TPIN generation is linked to PACRA registration; check the email for credentials and complete tax-type registration in the ZRA account.

ZRA business requirements ↗
ZRA · Individual TPIN

ZRA provides a Get a TPIN route. Its guidance identifies identity and address information/documents that may be required. Requirements depend on taxpayer type.
